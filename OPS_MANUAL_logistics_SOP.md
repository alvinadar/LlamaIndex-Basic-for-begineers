MOTOR-01 ACCIDENT REPORTING
Any accident must be reported to the company within 24 hours of the incident.
A police report must be lodged within 24 hours for all accidents involving

Windscreen damage is covered under the windscreen endorsement only.
The excess payable for a windscreen claim is RM 100 or 10 percent of the
windscreen sum insured, whichever is higher. A windscreen claim does not
affect the No Claim Discount if the windscreen endorsement is active.

MOTOR-03 FLOOD AND SPECIAL PERILS
Flood, storm, and landslide damage are covered only if the Special Perils
endorsement was purchased. Without this endorsement, flood damage is excluded.
Vehicles driven into visibly flooded roads may have claims reduced or rejected
on grounds of negligence.

MOTOR-04 NO CLAIM DISCOUNT
The No Claim Discount starts at 25 percent after one claim-free year and
rises to a maximum of 55 percent after five claim-free years.
Any at-fault claim resets the discount to zero at the next renewal.

Flood damage to the building and contents is covered under the
Houseowner Special Perils section. Claims must be notified within 14 days.
Photographic evidence of the water level and damaged items is required.

HOME-02 THEFT AND BURGLARY
Theft is covered only where there is visible forcible entry to the premises.
A police report must be lodged within 24 hours. Cash is covered up to RM 500
per incident. Jewellery above RM 5000 must be declared individually.

HOME-03 CONTENTS VALUATION
Contents are insured on an indemnity basis unless the Replacement Cost
endorsement is purchased. Under indemnity, depreciation is deducted based
on the age and condition of the item at the time of loss.
